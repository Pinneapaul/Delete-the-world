Delete the World – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy explains how the game Delete the World ("the game"), published by What Would Happen, handles your information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The game has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or any other personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (completed challenges, stars and settings such as sound on/off) is saved only on your own device, using your browser’s local storage. It never leaves your device and is deleted if you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.

Video clips

If you use the REC button, the clip is created on your device and saved only where you choose. We never receive or upload your videos.

Third-party services

The game is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. When the game loads, these services may receive standard technical information such as your IP address, as with any website. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
